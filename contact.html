Frequently Asked Questions

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship our handloom fabrics worldwide. Shipping costs and delivery times vary based on the destination and order size.

Can I request a custom design?

Absolutely. We work closely with designers and boutiques to create bespoke patterns and color palettes. Please select "Custom Design" in the enquiry form above.

What is your minimum order quantity for bulk?

Our minimum order quantity for bulk pricing is 50 meters per fabric type. For custom designs, the minimum is 100 meters.

How can I visit your weavers?

We organize guided tours of our weaving clusters once a month. If you're interested in visiting, please send us a general enquiry and we will provide the schedule.
