Fabric Care Guide

Silk Care

Dry clean only. Store in a cool, dry place wrapped in pure cotton fabric. Avoid direct sunlight to maintain the vibrancy of the natural dyes.

Cotton & Linen Care

Hand wash in cold water with mild detergent. Do not wring or twist. Dry in shade. Iron while slightly damp for the best crisp finish.
