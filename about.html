About Us

Our history, our mission, and the artisans who make it all possible.

Our Story & Expertise

Founded in 1995, the Handloom Weavers Collective began as a small cooperative of 20 artisan families. Today, we are a thriving community of over 500 weavers dedicated to preserving the ancient art of handloom weaving.

Our expertise spans generations. We specialize in working with pure cotton, wild silk, and organic linen. By combining traditional techniques with contemporary designs, we create fabrics that are not only beautiful but also sustainable and ethically produced.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower handloom weavers by providing them with a fair trade platform, ensuring sustainable livelihoods, and bringing authentic, high-quality handcrafted textiles to the global market.

Our Vision

A world where traditional craftsmanship is celebrated, where weavers are paid fairly for their art, and where sustainable fashion becomes the norm rather than the exception.

Our Core Values

Heritage

We honor and preserve ancient weaving techniques passed down through generations.

Integrity

We operate with complete transparency in our pricing, sourcing, and labor practices.

Quality

We never compromise on the quality of our yarns, dyes, or the final woven fabric.

The Weaver Community

The Handloom Weavers Collective is more than just a business; it is a family. We invest back into our communities by funding local schools, setting up healthcare camps, and providing continuous skill-development workshops.

When you choose our fabrics, you are directly contributing to the welfare and upliftment of entire artisan villages.
